# World city data

`ghsl-wup-r2025a-v1-1-2042.tsv` contains a 2042 snapshot derived from the European Commission Joint Research Centre's GHS-WUP-MTUC R2025A V1.1 urban-centre statistics.

- Source: https://human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu/ghs_wup_mtuc_r2025a.php
- Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.2905/1ea967e5-bedc-4cf3-a0b0-3851742ee7e2
- Original file: `GHS_WUP_MTUC_MT_GLOBE_R2025A_v1_1.xlsx`
- Coordinate reference system: EPSG:4326 (WGS84)
- License/reuse notice: https://human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu/GHSLhowToCite.php

Population, centroid, urban area, and built-up area values are linearly interpolated 40% of the way from the 2040 epoch to the 2045 epoch. Urban centres present in 2040 but absent in 2045 retain their 2040 values. The snapshot therefore contains the 14,227 urban centres present in 2040.

Required attribution: © European Union, 1995–2025. Source: GHSL, European Commission, Joint Research Centre.

Korean labels for capitals and urban centres with a 2042 population of at least one million are matched from Natural Earth 1:10m Populated Places `name_ko` values. Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
